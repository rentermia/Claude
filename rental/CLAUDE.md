# Car Rental LLC — notes for Claude

Everything in this folder belongs ONLY to the rental LLC. See `business-profile.md`.

Files:
- `business-profile.md` — legal name, EIN location, insurance, accounts, policies
- `tasks.md` — to-dos
- `fleet.md` — every vehicle, status, maintenance
- `rental-log.md` — every rental: dates, price, tax, owner payout, broker fee, net
- `monthly-costs.md` — car payments + insurance for own cars, and each car's profit/loss for the month
- `expenses.md` — expenses for this LLC only

When drafting customer messages, use the rental business name and its policies
(deposit, mileage, fuel, late return, age requirements) from `business-profile.md`.

How the money works: some cars are Renter's own (keep 100%); others belong to partner
owners who get a set payout per day (e.g. $420/day for the 2019 Uruses). Brokers often bring
the customer and get a fee. Always record price, tax, owner payout, broker fee and net.
