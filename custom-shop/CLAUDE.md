# Car Customization Shop LLC — notes for Claude

Everything in this folder belongs ONLY to the shop LLC. See `business-profile.md`.

Files:
- `business-profile.md` — legal name, services, pricing, warranty, accounts
- `tasks.md` — to-dos
- `jobs.md` — board of all jobs (one line each)
- `jobs/` — one file per job with payments, cost breakdown and profit (copy `jobs/_TEMPLATE.md`)
- `parts-and-suppliers.md` — parts on order and supplier contacts
- `monthly-costs.md` — recurring monthly costs (rent share, utilities, etc.)
- `expenses.md` — expenses for this LLC only

If a job is on one of the rental LLC's cars, it is an inter-company job — also log it in
`../between-businesses/log.md`.

For every job, track: price to client, payments received, balance due at pickup, each cost
line, total cost, profit and margin. Update both the job file and the line on `jobs.md`.
